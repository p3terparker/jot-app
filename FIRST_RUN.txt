Jot 3.0 — start here

INSTALL
1. Drag Jot.app onto Applications.
2. Eject this disk image, then open Jot from Applications.
3. Jot lives in your menu bar. Click its icon or press Option-Command-J.
   Your first note shows what Jot can do.

This preview is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch,
open System Settings > Privacy & Security and look for Open Anyway after trying
to open Jot. Approve only the copy you received from a trusted source.
If that option is unavailable, ask for a signed/notarized installer.
No Terminal commands or disabling system security are required by Jot.

WRITING
Open Jot and type. Notes save as you go. Notes use Markdown: # headings,
**bold**, *italic*, lists, [ ] checklists, links and tables look formatted while
you type. The Aa button shows a formatting bar; Command-/ shows the plain
Markdown. The list button opens your notes: search them, pin them, drag to
reorder, or press Command-Shift-Delete to delete one (Undo is offered).
Deleted notes stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

SENDING A NOTE
The Share menu sends the open note to Apple Notes or Stickies, saves it as text,
Markdown, rich text, HTML or PDF, opens the system Share sheet, or copies it.
- Apple Notes asks once for Automation permission.
- Stickies needs Accessibility permission for Jot (System Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Accessibility). Without it Jot copies the note, opens Stickies and
  tells you what to press.
- Reminders (the bell) need notification permission, which macOS asks for when
  you set the first one.

YOUR NOTES STAY ON YOUR MAC
Each note is a plain .md file in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Jot/Notes
(the ... menu has Show Notes in Finder). Jot makes no network connections and
has no account. Back up that folder like any other documents.

SETTINGS
Open the ... menu > Settings for the shortcut (Option-Command-J for new
installs; an install that never chose one keeps Control-J), open at login (off
until you turn it on), font, size, background, formatted or Markdown display,
spell checking and which Share items to show.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 13 or newer. Apple Silicon and Intel are both included. Intel and
macOS 13 hardware acceptance is still pending.

UPDATES
Download a newer installer, quit Jot, drag the new app into Applications and
choose Replace. Your notes and settings stay in place.

UNINSTALL
Quit Jot and drag it to the Trash. Your notes stay in the folder above until
you delete them.
